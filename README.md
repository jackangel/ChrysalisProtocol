**Edition 1.1** · November 2026 · Public Consultation Edition  
**Authorship:** Conceived and directed by me with AI-assisted research and drafting (see §4 for full methodology and limitations).

## 1. What This Is

Chrysalis is a blueprint for **human self-governance during the greatest technological shift in history**. 

It is a practical architecture designed to solve a riddle that modern politics has failed to answer: **How do ordinary human beings maintain sovereign control over their lives when artificial intelligence becomes the most capable form of cognition on Earth?**

The project starts from a clear observation and an urgent decision:

- **The Core Reality:** Every political and economic system we rely on—representative democracies, markets, courts, and bureaucracies—was engineered around three assumptions:
  1. Information is scarce and expensive to gather.
  2. Human labor is indispensable to economic production.
  3. No single actor or system can out-think the rest of humanity combined.
  
  **All three assumptions are now expiring simultaneously.** Our institutions are not struggling because corrupt or foolish people run them; they are breaking because they were never built to withstand infinite synthetic information, cognitive systems that outmatch median professionals, and the collapse of labor's economic leverage. 

- **The Fundamental Decision:** If we are moving into an era of autonomous cognitive systems and robotics, **who sets the rules?**  
  Right now, the default answer is simple: a handful of private tech monopolies, supervised by an aging political class that neither understands the technology nor commands the public's trust. The alternative cannot be a reactionary retreat into technocracy or authoritarian control. The rules of this transition must be written openly, deliberately, and democratically by the people who have to live with the consequences.

Chrysalis is that alternative: a transitional, open-source democratic protocol that any municipality or community can adopt. It pairs the analytical horsepower of multi-model AI with the incorruptible common sense of ordinary citizens selected by lottery. It is built to raise the intelligence of public decisions without surrendering human sovereignty, enforces total transparency on every line of code and budget, and includes an explicit, binding mechanism for its own dissolution.

Crucially, **Chrysalis is an explicitly temporary scaffold—a transition protocol designed to last only until Artificial Superintelligence (ASI) is achieved**. 
- Once true ASI arrives—an intelligence operating orders of magnitude beyond biological capability across all cognitive, scientific, and administrative domains—human administrative governance will become obsolete. A completely new, post-human governance system (most likely 99% or 100% operated by aligned superintelligence) will be required to coordinate planetary resource flows, ecology, energy grids, and material abundance.
- The fundamental purpose of Chrysalis is to **safely bridge the gap** between today's vulnerable human institutions and that ASI horizon: preventing private corporate capture, securing an unconditional economic floor (Universal High Income) before labor value goes negative, and establishing constitutional gates so that when full ASI governance emerges, it does so under democratic human charter rather than as the totalitarian arm of a tech monopoly.
- If machine intelligence stalls or advances slowly, what remains is still immensely valuable: a battle-tested, capture-resistant civic deliberation platform that frees communities from partisan paralysis and guarantees an unconditional basic income floor.

## 2. What This Is Not

To be completely clear from the start:

- **It is NOT a permanent world government or an eternal bureaucracy.** It is a temporary protocol designed to self-dissolve when its transitional mission is accomplished.
- **It is NOT an argument that current AI should rule us.** Today's narrow AI models are entirely unfit to govern. Under Chrysalis, AI acts exclusively as an analytical tool, researcher, and procedural moderator. Sovereign authority stays 100% in human hands until true ASI arrives, meets rigorous safety criteria, and receives supermajority public consent to take over operational governance. Even then, the public retains the permanent right to say: *"No, never."*
- **It is NOT a political party or a corporation.** It is an open-source civic protocol—a set of verifiable rules, open software, and model statutes that any sovereign town, city, or region can adopt voluntarily, run locally, or abandon at will.
- **It is NOT finished dogma.** This document suite is a public invitation to critique, debate, stress-test, and improve. Every empirical claim carries an explicit certainty rating, and every open question is accompanied by a transparent interim rule.

## 4. How This Was Created: Radical Transparency

This text was drafted by Manuel Barca-Grama using artificial intelligence systems to simulate multidisciplinary expert criticism (constitutional design, AI alignment, labor economics, cryptoeconomics, complexity science, and international law). 

We state this plainly because a project dedicated to institutional integrity must tell the truth about its own origins:

- **AI Simulations Are Not Human Endorsements.** No prestigious academic or institutional authority has stamped their approval on this text yet. The AI panels were used like an aerodynamic wind tunnel: to throw brutal counter-arguments at the design and find where the struts buckled before exposing it to the real world.
- **The First Priority Is Human Expert Scrutiny.** We do not ask you to trust an AI-assisted draft. The very first goal of our funding campaign is to pay genuine, independent human experts to attack this architecture in public and publish their unedited findings.
- **Auditable Evidence.** All empirical claims in [02_Evidence_Base.md](02_Evidence_Base.md) cite published, peer-reviewed academic literature or verified statistical databases. Every claim is labeled by confidence level so readers know what is established science and what is an active hypothesis.
- **No Hidden Conflicts.** Budget ranges are conservative estimates. Model statutes are templates requiring local legal adaptation. The founder’s compensation is fixed, public, and strictly capped.

## 5. The Five Constitutional Commitments

Every component of Chrysalis is anchored by five non-negotiable structural commitments:

1. **Sunlight by Default:** Every major civic decision, line of expenditure, and prompt/response used in public AI analysis is published immediately to a tamper-proof public ledger. Government secrecy is treated as an extraordinary, narrow exception, never the norm.
2. **Structural Immunity to Oligarchy:** Power is never accumulated in permanent offices. Representatives are chosen by random lottery (sortition) for strictly limited terms. Most importantly, **the founder’s executive authority expires automatically at month 24 by statute and code**.
3. **Provenance, Not Infallibility:** The public ledger records *who claimed what, when, and on what evidence*. It never claims to be an all-knowing oracle. Truth is approached through open, contestable evidence grading that any citizen can challenge.
4. **Unconditional Human Sovereignty:** AI models provide research, digest technical data, and draft options slates. **Only human citizens vote.** Any eventual delegation of narrow executive tasks across the Bridge requires meeting unforgiving safety criteria, surviving adversarial audits, and passing a supermajority public vote.
5. **Designed for Obsolescence:** Chrysalis is a temporary scaffold for a turbulent historical transition. It contains explicit legal and technical triggers for its own peaceful dissolution.

## 6. How to Challenge and Break This Design

This architecture only deserves to survive if it can withstand hostile scrutiny. We actively invite you to attack it:

- **The Public Objection Protocol:** Submit formal critiques to any claim, mechanism, or legal drafting. Every substantiated critique receives a public answer on the ledger, and the most compelling challenges will be incorporated directly into future revisions.
- **Pre-Registered Falsifiers:** In [07_Design_Rationale.md](07_Design_Rationale.md) §4, we list the precise empirical conditions that would invalidate our premises. If the data proves our assumptions wrong, we will publish the retraction openly.
- **Open Source and Forkable:** Every document, schema, and line of code is published under open-source licenses. If you believe you can design a fairer, safer transition protocol, fork the repository and build it. Our loyalty is to human freedom, not to our specific brand.
